An Italian villa on the Riviera, the way it used to be

We fell in love with the view and the garden. From the living room and the terrace you look across the bay of Beaulieu to the Cap Ferrat peninsula, with the yachts at anchor below and the sea changing colour through the day.

The villa has four bedrooms and three bathrooms: three bedrooms with sea views, plus a fourth with its own shower room that works well for children. Inside, Riviera style meets contemporary comfort — a fully equipped open kitchen, a large sitting room opening onto the terrace, air conditioning in every room, and a video-monitored alarm.

Outside, a terraced Mediterranean garden with a flat lawn, mature trees and flowering shrubs, a heated pool long enough for laps, and a shaded dining terrace for the long evenings. The beach is 200 metres away and the shops and restaurants of Beaulieu a ten-minute walk — with a lift as an alternative to the steep street.
